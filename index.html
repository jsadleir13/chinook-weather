How to read this

EC = Environment Canada's official forecast, written by its forecasters (about 6 days ahead). Used as the headline number when available.

Models = the median of 7 computer models: Canada HRDPS (2.5 km), RDPS (10 km), GDPS, ECMWF, ECMWF-AI, GFS/HRRR and ICON. Add Google's forecast in ⚙ settings.

Dot colour = how well the models agree on the high: ● within 3°, ● 4–6°, ● 7° or more. Red usually means a chinook or arctic front is in play and timing is uncertain.

High = 6 AM–9 PM. Low = that night (6 PM–9 AM), the same way Environment Canada reports it.
